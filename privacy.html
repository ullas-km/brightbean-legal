Privacy Policy

BrightBean Studio is a social media management application that helps users create, manage, schedule, and publish content to connected social media platforms.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process account details, content you create or upload, connected social media account information, and information needed to operate and secure the application.

Google and YouTube Data

If you connect a Google or YouTube account, BrightBean Studio accesses only the information and permissions authorized by you. This may include channel information and the ability to upload videos or perform other YouTube actions enabled by your granted permissions.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide requested features, manage connected accounts, publish content at your direction, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve the service.

Storage and Security

We use appropriate safeguards to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the application or disclosed when required by law. Content you choose to publish may be visible to the relevant social media audience.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. To request deletion of your information, contact us using the email below. You can also revoke Google access through your Google Account permissions settings.

Third-Party Services

Connected platforms, including Google and YouTube, process data according to their own privacy policies and terms. Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
